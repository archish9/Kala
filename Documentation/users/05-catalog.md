# Catalog

Every style, colour palette, and font pairing kala can draw on when it bootstraps a design
system. You do not pick from these directly — you describe your product and kala matches
against them — but this is the full list of what it is matching against.

These are the **fallback tier**. kala prefers its 12 hand-authored systems
([Design systems](06-design-systems.md)) and only reaches for this catalog when none of
those fit your brief well. See
[the catalog fallback tier](06-design-systems.md#the-catalog-fallback-tier).

Ported from [ui-ux-pro-max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) under
MIT — see [Provenance](../contributors/07-provenance.md).

---

light and dark. That is why there is one accent colour listed rather than a full swatch set.
